# Stage 2 — style-frame briefs

These are not promotional key art. Each frame is a prospective film shot and must prove character, relationship, material language, depth, and composition.

## SF-01 — First appearance / blank page

**Moment:** the user has typed the first mark; GPT is present because the conversation began.

**Composition:** medium-wide 16:9 shared studio. Pale worktable runs diagonally from lower-left to mid-right. GPT sits/leans at the near side, turned three-quarters toward the empty opposite chair. A single sunflower-yellow insertion point blinks on a large off-white sheet placed from the far/user side. The user's chair is slightly pulled back as if someone just sat down. Foreground: out-of-focus ruler/paper edge crossing one lower corner. Background: quiet shelves and diffuse morning window.

**GPT acting:** attentive, slight forward lean, one hand resting near—but not touching—the user's page. No smile yet; eyes on the insertion point.

**Colour:** almost entirely paper/graphite. Cobalt appears only in the tiny ribbon/brooch. Sunflower insertion point is the strongest accent.

**Pass test:** thumbnail must read “two people are about to work together” even though only one person is visible.

## SF-02 — World-making / paper city

**Moment:** a simple idea has become unexpectedly large and concrete.

**Composition:** match from top-down desk into an oblique WORLD scene: a folded paper city physically rises from a map across the table and extends past the room boundary. Buildings are cut paper with printed windows and graphite construction lines; one street remains a drawn line on the original sheet. GPT stands on the studio side, lifting one paper block into place. A sunflower cursor/selection rectangle from the user has just moved one tiny building, and the surrounding city adapts around that intervention.

**Depth:** foreground paper tower edge, midground GPT + city, background studio dissolving into illustrated sky.

**GPT acting:** focused but delighted by scale; body follows the object she is placing.

**No:** holograms, glowing grids, futuristic skyline, floating data.

## SF-03 — Play / absurd collaborative construction

**Moment:** user and GPT are improvising together and both understand the joke.

**Composition:** bright table crowded with a beautiful impossible object: e.g. a tiny greenhouse riding on library wheels while a paper whale passes through its roof, all built from editorial collage. GPT is standing, sleeves slightly rolled, holding a strip of paper that becomes the whale's tail. The user cursor has pinned a sunflower sticky note reading only a short scene-native instruction. Several earlier rejected pieces lie nearby as harmless scraps.

**GPT acting:** small asymmetric smile, eyes glancing toward the user's latest intervention rather than at camera.

**Rhythm clue:** objects at different stages of settling imply that this shot will have staggered secondary motion.

**Pass test:** should feel inventive and human, not “AI can generate anything” advertising.

## SF-04 — Misread / the bridge misses

**Moment:** GPT confidently built the wrong interpretation and recognises it.

**Composition:** a paper suspension bridge constructed from a sentence spans from the table toward a clipped reference image—but its final section visibly lands at the wrong target. The user has drawn one vermilion circle/arrow around the intended destination. GPT is frozen for one beat with a strip still in her hand, eyes tracking from the failed landing to the user's correction. Sunflower user mark remains present; cobalt construction marks show what GPT thought the plan was.

**Colour:** vermilion enters for the first time, only in editorial correction marks. No red lighting.

**Expression:** realization, not shame or horror.

**Pass test:** viewer must understand the mistake visually before reading any text.

## SF-05 — Revision / proof layers align

**Moment:** wrong drafts become useful material for a better answer.

**Composition:** several large transparent proof sheets hang/float physically between camera and GPT, each carrying a different graphite/cobalt draft of the same object. They are offset in perspective like tracing paper on pins. GPT moves the last sheet into alignment; where layers agree, a crisp corrected illustration appears. The user has placed a sunflower registration mark at one corner, making alignment a shared act.

**Depth:** foreground transparent sheet edge and grease-pencil note; midground aligned drawing + hands; background GPT face and studio.

**Expression:** concentrated, calm, slight relief.

**No:** glitch, RGB split, digital scanlines.

## SF-06 — Final quiet frame / room to continue

**Moment:** the session has produced something real, but the page is intentionally unfinished.

**Composition:** late-afternoon shared desk, calmer and less crowded. GPT sits side-on at the table, relaxed, looking at the page rather than camera. Most finished artifacts have been stacked to one side. In the center is one large off-white sheet with a nearly complete editorial composition. One cobalt folded-ribbon mark and one sunflower user mark are both active near the unfinished lower-right corner. The opposite chair is now pulled in. Soft foreground paper edge frames the scene.

**Colour:** warm paper/graphite, restrained cobalt and sunflower, one tiny soft-green completion mark somewhere already finished. No vermilion.

**Final feeling:** not goodbye, not loneliness, not triumph. The work can continue after the cut.

- user presence obvious without explanation?;
- >= 3 depth layers?;
- tactile/material read?;
- palette semantics correct?;
- any generic AI/cyber visual residue?;
- pass / revise / reject.
