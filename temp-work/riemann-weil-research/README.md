## Research question

Primary target: determine whether finite-dimensional compressions of the Weil Hermitian form reveal a new route toward proving or disproving global Weil positivity, especially through spectral/inertia structure as support crosses prime-power thresholds.

Immediate subproblems:

1. Reconstruct the exact finite-dimensional reduction used in the 2026 Alpöge–Furman work and related compact-support positivity results.
2. Identify which information is discarded by trace/second-moment bounds and whether full-spectrum or Schur-complement information can improve the argument.
3. Test candidate monotonicity/no-crossing statements for the smallest eigenvalue as support grows.
4. Search adversarially for explicit legal test functions with negative Weil quadratic form; any numerical candidate must later be converted into rigorous interval/exact bounds.
5. Record failed conjectures explicitly so they are not rediscovered.
