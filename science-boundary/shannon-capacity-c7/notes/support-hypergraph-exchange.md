# Support-hypergraph formulation for unrestricted C7^10 exchanges

For a fixed independent set `I` in `C7^10`, define the blocker support of any outside vertex `v` by

`S(v) = N[v] ∩ I`.

A net `+2` exchange of radius `r` exists exactly when there are `r+2` pairwise nonconfusable outside vertices whose support union has size at most `r`. Any unused deletion budget can be padded with arbitrary old vertices. Thus the local improvement problem is naturally a constrained independent-set problem over candidate vertices labelled by small support hyperedges, rather than a geometric Hamming-ball search around the original BPZ construction.

For the FunSearch t=9 one-hit deletion seeds, full-space transforms give `coverage0=0`. Exact unrestricted searches have now ruled out net `+2` through support-union size 5 on all four C5 orbit representatives, hence on all 40 one-hit states.

## Why support connectivity matters

For an owner set `U`, write `P(U)` for every outside vertex with support contained in `U`. A negative radius-`s` result implies `alpha(P(U)) <= s+1` for every `|U| <= s`: otherwise an independent `s+2`-set in `P(U)` would be a net `+2` exchange after padding deletions.

The representative seeds satisfy stronger replayed bounds:

- every one-owner pool has independence number at most 1 (`heavy_c1_owners=0`);
- every genuine two-owner pool has independence number at most 2;
- a dedicated saturated-triple census on all four C5 representatives gives independence number at most 3 for every three-owner pool (`productive_alpha4_triples=0` on every representative);
- exact radius-4 negativity gives every four-owner pool independence number at most 5;
- exact radius-5 negativity gives every five-owner pool independence number at most 6.

At radius 4, if six selected additions use four owners and their support hypergraph is disconnected, the owner partition is either `3+1` or `2+2`; the component bounds give at most `3+1=4` or `2+2=4`. Therefore any radius-4 witness without a coverage-4 candidate must have a connected support graph across all four owners.

This yields a complete owner-quadruple generator:

1. direct support of a coverage-4 candidate;
2. union of two coverage-3 supports that expose four owners;
3. a coverage-3 support plus a crossing coverage-2 support;
4. with only coverage-one/two candidates, a connected coverage-two support graph generated by a two-edge wedge plus an extending edge.

For each generated owner quadruple, replay the complete candidate pool from all support groups contained in it and solve the induced independent-set problem exactly for six additions.

## Radius 5

Once radius 4 is negative, every disconnected partition of five owners supports at most six additions under the certified component bounds. Therefore a seven-addition radius-5 witness must have a connected support hypergraph unless it contains a direct coverage-5 support, which is handled separately.

The exact radius-5 search has two complementary complete stages:

1. direct owner-five supports coming from coverage-5 candidates;
2. connected lower-support owner-five sets generated from coverage-2/3/4 support expansions.

Both stages reconstruct the complete pool `P(U)` before solving the induced independent-set problem. All four C5 representatives are negative in both stages, so unrestricted remove-5/add-7 is exactly closed for the FunSearch one-hit deletion family.

## Radius 6 Hall-type constraints

Consider a putative radius-6 witness: eight pairwise nonconfusable additions whose total owner union is a six-set `U`.

For any subset `T ⊆ U`, additions whose supports avoid `T` all lie in `P(U \ T)`. Hence

`# additions whose support intersects T >= 8 - alpha(P(U \ T))`.

Using the replayed exact bounds gives:

- every individual owner must occur in the support of at least 2 selected additions, because the other five owners support at most 6;
- every owner pair must be met by at least 3 selected additions, because the other four owners support at most 5;
- every owner triple must be met by at least 5 selected additions, because the complementary three-owner pool has independence number at most 3.

These are necessary incidence constraints and should be enforced before solving a six-owner candidate pool.

### All disconnected radius-6 patterns are impossible

The exact saturated-triple census removes the former `3+3` exception. For every disconnected partition of six owners, add the certified component bounds:

- `5+1`: at most `6+1=7`;
- `4+2`: at most `5+2=7`;
- `4+1+1`: at most `5+1+1=7`;
- `3+3`: at most `3+3=6`;
- all finer partitions are no larger.

Therefore any radius-6 remove-6/add-8 witness must have a **connected** support hypergraph on all six owners.

The exact radius-6 decomposition is consequently:

1. selected support of size 6: handled by `code/search_net2_r6_direct.cpp`;
2. no size-6 support but at least one selected size-5 support: handled by `code/search_net2_r6_c5_anchor.cpp` using Hall-forced co-owner attachment to the sixth owner;
3. all selected supports have size at most 4: still requires a complete connected lower-support generator. A complete generator may anchor a selected size-4 support and expand by crossing observed supports until all six owners appear; if no size-4 support is selected, repeat from size-3 and then size-2 anchors. Every generated owner-six set must be deduplicated, its complete `P(U)` reconstructed, and the Hall incidence inequalities checked before exact independent-8 search.

## Exact support recovery without high-order moments

For radius 6 and beyond, `code/census_support6_lists.cpp` uses an in-place exact support-list transform. Store, at every one of the `7^10` ambient vertices, the sorted list of blocker-owner labels while its size is at most six, otherwise an overflow marker. Expanding one coordinate replaces a record by the union of the three cyclic neighboring records.

Support size is monotone during the ten coordinate expansions. Therefore any final ambient vertex with blocker coverage at most six never overflowed earlier, and its complete owner support is recovered exactly. With six `uint32` labels per ambient vertex, the main array uses about 6.8 GB decimal while avoiding high-order moment inversion.

## Completeness discipline

The invariant to preserve is completeness: computational failure only excludes the owner/support families actually generated. Every reduction from all owner sets to a smaller support family needs an explicit combinatorial argument using already-certified lower-radius independence bounds. Stronger properties must be replayed or checked for every symmetry representative on which they are used; they must not be silently extrapolated from case 0.
