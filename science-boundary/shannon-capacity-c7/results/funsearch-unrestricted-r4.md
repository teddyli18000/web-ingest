## Complete four-owner support decomposition

For a six-addition witness supported on four old owners:

1. a selected coverage-4 candidate directly exposes its owner quadruple;
2. with coverage-3 candidates but no coverage-4 candidate, the fourth owner cannot occur only in one coverage-1 candidate, because a three-owner pool contributes at most four vertices; therefore either two coverage-3 supports expose the union of four owners or a crossing coverage-2 support exposes the fourth owner;
3. with only coverage-one/two candidates, their multi-owner support graph must be connected. A `3+1` component split contributes at most `4+1=5`; a `2+2` split contributes at most `2+2=4`. Every connected graph on four owners has a spanning tree, generated by a two-edge wedge plus one extending edge.

For every generated owner quadruple, `search_net2_r4.cpp` forms the complete pool of all coverage-one, -two, -three, and -four candidates whose supports are contained in that quadruple and searches it exactly for an independent six-set.
