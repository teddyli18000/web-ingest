# 2026-10-28 near-skew structure and exact shells

## Observation

The public `N=100, E=578` incumbent has the following exact autocorrelation absolute-value counts:

```text
|C| = 0: 12
|C| = 1: 50
|C| = 2: 16
|C| = 4: 16
|C| = 6: 4
|C| = 8: 1
```

All 50 odd lags have `|C_k|=1`, the parity-forced minimum for an even-length sequence. They therefore contribute exactly `50` to the energy; all remaining `528` comes from even lags.

This is explained by the historical `N=99` skew-symmetric `E=577` sequence. Prepending `+1` to that sequence gives the `N=100` incumbent exactly. If `t` is length-99 skew-symmetric, then every odd autocorrelation of `t` vanishes. For `s=(+1,t)`,

```text
C_k(s) = t_{k-1} + C_k(t),
```

so every odd `C_k(s)` is automatically `±1`.

This defines a 50-free-variable `N=100` near-skew subspace of size `2^50` in which all odd-lag energy is already minimal.

## Heuristic probes

A 50-variable tabu search was calibrated both from random near-skew starts and perturbations of the incumbent. Incumbent perturbations routinely returned to `E=578`, but random starts remained far from the record (representative 64-run, 20,000-step batch: best `1166`). A self-avoiding-walk probe modeled on the core `lssOrel` mechanism was also poor in this transformed objective; representative 32-run batches with one million neighbor probes per run had best energies `1166`, `1202`, and `1218` for walk lengths 200, 400, and 800.

These results say only that the first simple heuristics do not globally solve this near-skew subspace efficiently. They do not rule out a better sequence inside it.

## Exact symmetry-to-symmetry crossover cube

Among the eight standard LABS symmetry images of the incumbent, exactly one other image also lies in the same `s=(+1, skew99)` near-skew representation. In the 50 free coordinates it differs from the incumbent in exactly the 25 even-indexed variables

```text
0,2,4,...,48.
```

Therefore the complete recombination space between these two energy-578 endpoints contains only `2^25 = 33,554,432` sequences. A Gray-code scan enumerated every point of this crossover subcube while updating correlations exactly.

Result:

```text
CUBE tested=33554432 best=578 seconds=8.95783
```

The only global minimum energy observed in the cube was `578`; no `574` candidate exists anywhere in this entire symmetry-to-symmetry recombination space.

## Related negative probes

- Searching ordinary fixed Hamming-distance shells heuristically by swap moves was poorly calibrated.
- Splitting the sequence into even and odd subsequences makes all odd-lag correlations linear in either half when the other half is fixed. Tabu repair can restore odd-lag energy `50` after small perturbations, but the resulting full energies remained substantially above record quality; representative bests were about `774` after one-half-bit perturbations and worse for larger perturbations.
- Alternating half-sequence local search reached only about `894`.
- Elite crossover among the eight standard symmetry images repeatedly returned only those same eight images; no inequivalent `E=578` solution appeared.
- Diverse skew-99 candidates found by short tabu search transferred to N=100 at best `650` outside the known skew-99 optimum. Unrestricted cyclic-rotation priority-queue refinement of six such `650-686` seeds improved two `662` seeds only to `646` and otherwise retained the seed energy, still well above record quality.

## Interpretation

The useful conclusion is structural rather than a record improvement: the public incumbent is exactly the endpoint extension of the historical skew-99 optimum and sits inside a natural 50-variable manifold where every odd lag is already optimal. Direct local movement on that manifold is nevertheless strongly penalized, and even the complete `2^25` crossover cube between its two near-skew symmetry representatives contains no improvement. Future use of the near-skew restriction should therefore emphasize globally different proposals or stronger combinatorial optimization rather than small perturbations or symmetry recombination of the incumbent.
