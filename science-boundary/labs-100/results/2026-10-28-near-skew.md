# 2026-10-28 near-skew structure and exact shells

## Correction notice

An audit on 2026-10-28 found an off-by-one error in the first version of `code/nss_shell.cpp`. The length-99 skew tail occupies full-sequence indices `1..99`, so tail free variable `v` must be paired with full-sequence index `99-v` (with `v=49` the center bit). The old code incorrectly used `100-v`; for `v=0` that was even out of bounds.

Therefore the previously recorded near-skew shell minima `1266, 1398, 1382, 1338, 1314` for radii 4..8 and the old radius-8 Action run `40608557906` are **invalid and superseded**. They must not be used as evidence.

The scanner is now fixed and contains a startup invariant check: every single free-coordinate flip must preserve odd-lag energy exactly `50`, and its incremental score must equal a full recomputation. Corrected exact results are below. The complete symmetry-to-symmetry crossover-cube result was independently rerun after the fix and remains valid.

## Observation

The public `N=100, E=578` incumbent has the following exact autocorrelation absolute-value counts:

```text
|C| = 0: 12
|C| = 1: 50
|C| = 2: 16
|C| = 4: 16
|C| = 6: 4
|C| = 8: 1
```

All 50 odd lags have `|C_k|=1`, the parity-forced minimum for an even-length sequence. They contribute exactly `50` energy; the other `528` comes from even lags.

This is explained by the historical `N=99` skew-symmetric `E=577` sequence. Prepending `+1` gives the `N=100` incumbent exactly. If `t` is length-99 skew-symmetric, then every odd autocorrelation of `t` vanishes. For `s=(+1,t)`,

```text
C_k(s) = t_{k-1} + C_k(t),
```

so every odd `C_k(s)` is automatically `±1`.

This defines a 50-free-variable `N=100` near-skew subspace of size `2^50` in which odd-lag energy is already minimal.

## Heuristic probes

A 50-variable tabu search was calibrated both from random near-skew starts and perturbations of the incumbent. Incumbent perturbations routinely returned to `E=578`, but random starts remained far from the record (representative 64-run, 20,000-step batch: best `1166`). A self-avoiding-walk probe modeled on the core `lssOrel` mechanism was also poor in this transformed objective; representative 32-run batches with one million neighbor probes per run had best energies `1166`, `1202`, and `1218` for walk lengths 200, 400, and 800.

These heuristic results are unaffected by the exact-shell indexing bug because they used their own near-skew construction rather than the faulty shell coordinate map.

## Related probes

- Searching ordinary fixed Hamming-distance shells heuristically by swap moves was poorly calibrated.
- Splitting the sequence into even and odd subsequences makes all odd-lag correlations linear in either half when the other half is fixed. Tabu repair can restore odd-lag energy `50` after perturbations, but the first half-repair experiments remained substantially above record quality.
- Alternating half-sequence local search reached only about `894`.
- Elite crossover among the eight standard symmetry images repeatedly returned those same images; no inequivalent `E=578` solution appeared.
- Diverse skew-99 candidates transferred to N=100 at best `650` in those early tests; later unrestricted odd-perfect search (documented separately) found materially stronger non-near-skew basins, so this old `650` value is no longer the best structural proposal outside the incumbent basin.

## Interpretation

The robust structural fact is that the incumbent is exactly the endpoint extension of the historical skew-99 optimum and lies in a natural 50-variable manifold with minimal odd-lag energy. The corrected exact scan rules out improvement through free-coordinate radius 7, and the complete 25-coordinate symmetry crossover cube has exact minimum 578. The earlier stronger-looking shell-barrier numbers were artifacts of an indexing bug and are explicitly withdrawn.
