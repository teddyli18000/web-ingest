## K21 arithmetic without enumerating U multisets

For

```text
U_r=(d_r+d_(50-r))/2,
V_r=(d_r-d_(50-r))/2,
```

and `K=21`, the center is `m=K-25=-4`.  Hence

```text
sum U_r = 30,
W = 16 + 2 sum(U_r^2+V_r^2).
```

The `(18,0)` product class has `E=50+4W == 2 (mod 8)`, so a nominal `E<=574` breakthrough actually has `E<=570`, equivalently

```text
W<=130,
sum(U^2+V^2)<=57.                       (1)
```

For the order-25 compressed pair define

```text
G_j = PAF_p(j)+PAF_c(j) = U_j+U_(25-j),  j=1,...,12.
```

For two integers with fixed sum `g`,

```text
min_{u+v=g}(u^2+v^2) = ceil(g^2/2).
```

Therefore

```text
qmin(G) = sum_{j=1}^{12} ceil(G_j^2/2)
```

is the exact minimum possible `sum U^2` compatible with the compressed PAF.  A compressed candidate can satisfy (1) only if

```text
qmin(G) <= 57.                              (2)
```

This replaces explicit enumeration of dozens of `U` multisets.  As an independent regression, applying the same formula to the already closed `K=19` layer with its budget `47` reproduces exactly the twelve K19 sorted spectral types and their zero/two-defect fibre budgets.

## Stronger full-fibre invariant

For a reconstructed binary length-50 parity half, let `PAF_A(r),PAF_B(r)` denote cyclic autocorrelations.  Exact projection algebra gives

```text
4 U_r = PAF_A(r)+PAF_B(r),   r=1,...,24.    (3)
```

Thus define

```text
Q = sum_{r=1}^{24} U_r^2.                   (4)
```

`Q` is invariant under the complete 500-element affine image family used in the order-25 search:

- cyclic translation leaves cyclic PAF unchanged;
- multiplication of indices by an odd unit modulo 50 merely permutes the nonzero lag pairs.

Consequently an entire affine orbit of a full fibre lift is excluded at once whenever

```text
Q > 57.                                     (5)
```

If `Q<=57`, the exact remaining antisymmetric budget is not the compressed lower-bound budget `57-qmin(G)` but the sharper

```text
sum V^2 <= 57-Q.                            (6)
```

The full-fibre verifier therefore computes `Q` once per binary lift before generating any affine image.
