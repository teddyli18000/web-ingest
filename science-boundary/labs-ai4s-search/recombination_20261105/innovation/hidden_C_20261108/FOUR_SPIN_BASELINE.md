# Four-spin isolated subsystem classical reference — protocol frozen 2026-11-09

Motivated by Lau & Gangat, [Competitive scaling of small-subsystem-optimization heuristics for LABS](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.14361), arXiv:2610.14361v3 (2026-10-29). The paper reports competitive scaling on N27–60 and proposes complement testing, kicked descent, tracked subsystem selection and Y50 multi-subsystem proposals. Our implementation is paper-inspired, **not** a faithful certified reproduction of its state-of-the-art time-to-solution claims.

**Exact 4-spin isolated Hamiltonian:** E=∑_k(∑_i s_i s_(i+k))². Discard constant diagonal terms and, for each chosen spin subset S of cardinality 4, retain only pair terms 2 s_i s_(i+k) s_j s_(j+k), i<j, when all four involved indices are in S (duplicate indices are naturally squared). Enumerate all 2⁴ assignments exactly, select a minimum, consider its complement and calculate original global LABS E to determine candidate. Independent direct scorer and full small-subsystem term validator required.

Six fixed treatment arms under identical 150ms per scenario and identical first direct full Tabu7 repair(X) charged: KD n4 with X=80 failures from (0) repaired X; (1) original X; (2) original X with anchor restart at X=80 stalls; MS n4 Y=50 candidates X=90 with tracked subset selection from (3) repaired X; (4) original X; (5) original X with anchor restart after X=90 stalls. Keep same scenario input without C, model not used, C only in an external independent scorer **after** search.

Primary hidden C orbit and *any source-novel ≤5/10% canonical orbit*, plus parent symmetry-distance ≥ceil(N/5), repaired-energy quality, counts of isolated subsystems, wall time, on N46/47/50. AB_blind primary; C_shell is privileged C-conditioned diagnosis only. Training/validation/initial N50 target orbits are already exposed to design choices; this is source-independent *state-seed* exploration, not a new orbit holdout. An implementation failure does not reject all variants of the original published method, and any success only shows competitiveness of this particular code and budget.

This addresses the concern that a bare 64-step Tabu7/ILS baseline cannot represent the latest classical LABS research. Do not overclaim universal or modern state-of-the-art superiority. Preserve previous negative outcomes and exact SHA provenance.
