These mechanisms had distinct successes and fit the general property of
spherical defect lattices: global scar arrangement can change under symmetry
constraints without blowing up local elastic energy.

hop amplitude, new code combines *C3* six-temperature replica exchange
with a *C3* GA with registered patch recombination and true Deaven-Ho
spatial orbit cuts, while symmetry preserving. It uses compiled stable
Coulomb energy, C3 320-orbit representation, physical release of
low C3 minima up to R+0.55, real N960 scoring, independent three seeds.
