Question
--------
Does a closed path in the independent two-occupancy plane return to the same
endpoint charges on a different relaxed geometric branch?  Four rectangular
loops were tested, two scales and both traversal orders.  All 961 coordinates
were mobile at every occupancy step.  At each corner the state was projected
to genuine unit-charge N960 by deleting label886 or label960 and fully relaxing.

Key scientific result
---------------------
The auxiliary fractional-occupancy relaxation has a genuine nontrivial holonomy:
a closed parameter loop can return to exactly the same endpoint occupancy while
changing the relaxed genuine-N960 basin from R to A.  For the small rectangle,
this transport is order-dependent: 886-first returns R, while 960-first returns
A.  Thus the two occupancy operations are not effectively commutative after
adiabatic geometry relaxation.

This is stronger than the earlier one-dimensional endpoint hysteresis.  It
establishes a reproducible path-dependent basin-transport operator in an enlarged
search space, although the tested two-label loops only connect already-known R
and A.

Caveat / next reformulation
---------------------------
These rectangular loops do not conserve total fractional charge away from the
base point, so they are mechanism evidence rather than a clean constant-total
control.  The next experiment uses three distinguished deletion labels
886 (R), 960 (A), and 380 (higher 24-dislocation H380) with deficiencies summing
to one, hence total charge fixed exactly at 960 throughout.  Separate R/A/H
sheets are continued to a common occupancy-simplex center and projected back to
N960.  This tests whether the holonomy/multistability survives without a global
total-charge excursion and whether a third basin sheet gives a new exit.
