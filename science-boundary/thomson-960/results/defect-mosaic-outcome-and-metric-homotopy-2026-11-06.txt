Data notes: mosaic artifact result.json contains full coordinates of
up to 12 novel basins and the full gene/energy histories per seed.
Novelty was detected by simple energy classification; the same label
"novel" in separate seeds could be the *same* basin, so do not report
the nominal 30 detections as 30 distinct stationary points.
Grad RMS after quench typically 1e-6 to 1e-5; upper rows with larger
R residuals were not candidates and were not independent-polished.
The experimental operator still used fixed globally learned R→parent
fields and only varied 12 localized mixing weights, so plastic topology
changes incompatible with all donors may require different physics.

Reformulation: change the interaction landscape rather than only the
deformation geometry. Two parallel constructive families:
 - Riesz exponent r^-s homotopy (code/search_riesz_exponent_cycles.py,
   Action 42258720979), varying exponent to temporarily soften/harden
   Coulombic elastic penalties, then return to s=1 for final scoring.
 - anisotropic metric loops (code/search_anisotropic_metric_loops.py,
   Action 42261470927) locally relax with
       E_Q = sum[(x_i-x_j)^T Q (x_i-x_j)]^-1/2
   for SPD Q=exp(sym traceless S), traverse two noncommuting defect-centered
   distortions in a closed loop, then restore Q=I. This explicitly breaks
   rotational symmetry / moves the metric, and unlike the 12-core mosaic
   alters actual energies and basins during relaxation.
Both are evaluated on genuine 960-particle Coulomb energy after complete
return to original physical objective. Both save all numerically subrecord
candidate coordinates, to be independently polished and officially verified.
