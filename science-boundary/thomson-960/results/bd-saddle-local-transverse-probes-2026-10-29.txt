Method
------
The certified artifact was downloaded locally.  For each probe, the saddle was
perturbed by 0.012 RMS radians along one positive full-space physical Hessian
eigenmode (reaction bias zero) and fully unconstrained-relaxed with the same
stable long-double pair-energy-difference L-BFGS objective used by the durable
search code.  The purpose was rapid interface diagnosis while the Actions queue
was stalled, not verifier scoring.

Representation consequence
--------------------------
The minimal three-mode basis that already reaches all four known basins is
(mode1, mode2, mode6):
  mode1 distinguishes A/B,
  mode2 reaches D,
  mode6 distinguishes R/B.

A new search therefore treats coefficients in this 3D positive-mode subspace as
a reaction-coordinate space.  Space-filling samples are fully relaxed; Delaunay
simplices whose vertices map to different basins are refined adaptively, with
special priority on 3- and 4-basin junctions.  Small reaction-coordinate biases
are then evaluated around the most heterogeneous boundary points.  This changes
the search space from single eigenmodes to nonlinear mixed saddle-interface
coordinates.
