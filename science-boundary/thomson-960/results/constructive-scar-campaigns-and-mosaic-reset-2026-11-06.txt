STRUCTURAL SEARCH SPACE RESET:
  The main fixed assumption across these full-N960 campaigns was
  representing a scar rearrangement by one globally applied vector field
  or a small sum of globally applied fields. A nontrivial spatial pattern
  of distinct donor events *at each separate defect core* has not been
  optimized in these runs.
  New code: code/search_mosaic_scar_genomes.py
  New action: 42257750496, three independent 450-trial jobs.
  It assigns one of R, W, A, B, D2 C3-orbit displacement donors separately
  to each of twelve R scar cores, smoothly stitches the 12 local fields,
  and fully relaxes N960 using compiled Coulomb energy and gradient.
  Lightweight genotype crossover/mutation recycles low-energy mosaics;
  any sub-R numerical candidate is stored with all 960 point coordinates.
  Success criterion: genuinely lower final N960 energy, not number of
  excluded parameter cells or weighted-index saddles.
