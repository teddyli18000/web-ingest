Defect structure
----------------
Convex-hull/Delaunay coordination is 33 fivefold / 906 sixfold / 21 sevenfold, i.e. 21
dislocations with net topological charge 12.

The non-sixfold subgraph decomposes as:
- nine connected 5-7-5-7-5 scars (3 fivefold + 2 sevenfold each): 18 dislocations;
- three connected 5-7 dipoles: 3 dislocations;
- three isolated fivefold vertices.
Each isolated fivefold is about 0.1863010893 rad from one of the three 5-7 dipoles, and the
three such structures form a C3 orbit. Thus a useful structural description is nine full
scars plus three spatially broken short-scar structures.

Direct 21 -> 20 topology interface
----------------------------------
The e564 triangulation contains exactly nine flippable edges whose combinatorial edge flip
reduces total |coordination-6| by two and changes the coordination counts exactly

  33/906/21 -> 32/908/20.

All nine are 7-7 edges lying inside the nine full 5-7-5-7-5 scars. The opposite vertices
have degrees 5 and 6. Therefore one such flip converts one full two-dislocation scar into a
one-dislocation short scar. The resulting defect composition is exactly eight full scars
plus four short scars — the same 20-dislocation composition as the merged D2 record — but
starting from the distinct C3-derived global arrangement.

The nine flips form three distinct C3 orbits. A balanced spherical perturbation that moves
the new-diagonal endpoints together and old-diagonal endpoints apart crosses the actual
convex-hull flip boundary at amplitudes about 0.0179668, 0.0180433, and 0.0184463 rad for
the three orbits. Two local pilot relaxations started at 1.05 times the boundary healed back
to e564, so merely crossing the boundary is insufficient; larger and geometrically
different overshoots remain scientifically open.

Soft-mode / topology coupling
-----------------------------
An independent local Hessian recomputation reproduced the soft pair as
lambda0 = 5.20524653, lambda1 = 5.20524671. Under +120 degree rotation about the exact C3
axis, this two-dimensional eigenspace transforms as

  [[-1/2, +sqrt(3)/2], [-sqrt(3)/2, -1/2]]

with pair-subspace residual about 1.0e-6. Thus it is the expected non-trivial real C3
doublet and naturally selects one member of a threefold-equivalent structural set when C3
is broken.

The pair-subspace displacement weight on one-ring neighborhoods of the nine full scars is
not uniform. Grouped by the three C3 scar orbits, the weights are approximately
0.09482, 0.04250, and 0.03899 per scar. The highest-weight orbit is exactly the orbit whose
balanced Delaunay-flip threshold is lowest, about 0.0179668 rad. Hence the Hessian soft-mode
representation and the explicit Delaunay topology representation independently identify the
same easiest scar-shortening channel.
