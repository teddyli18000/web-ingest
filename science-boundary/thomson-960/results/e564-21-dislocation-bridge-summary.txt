All nine are 7-7 edges lying inside the nine full 5-7-5-7-5 scars. The opposite vertices

Soft-mode / topology coupling
-----------------------------
An independent local Hessian recomputation reproduced the soft pair as
lambda0 = 5.20524653, lambda1 = 5.20524671. Under +120 degree rotation about the exact C3
axis, this two-dimensional eigenspace transforms as

  [[-1/2, +sqrt(3)/2], [-sqrt(3)/2, -1/2]]

with pair-subspace residual about 1.0e-6. Thus it is the expected non-trivial real C3
doublet and naturally selects one member of a threefold-equivalent structural set when C3
is broken.

The pair-subspace displacement weight on one-ring neighborhoods of the nine full scars is
not uniform. Grouped by the three C3 scar orbits, the weights are approximately
0.09482, 0.04250, and 0.03899 per scar. The highest-weight orbit is exactly the orbit whose
balanced Delaunay-flip threshold is lowest, about 0.0179668 rad. Hence the Hessian soft-mode
representation and the explicit Delaunay topology representation independently identify the
same easiest scar-shortening channel.

Projection of an infinitesimal local balanced flip onto the soft pair is small in absolute
norm because the Delaunay move is highly localized: roughly 1.8e-4 of squared displacement
for the easiest orbit, 7.5e-5 for the hardest, and 7.2e-6 for the middle orbit. Even the
first eight Hessian modes explain less than about 1e-3 of the local flip field. Thus the
mechanistic picture is two-scale: the soft global doublet selects which scar family is most
susceptible, while the actual topology change requires high-curvature localized degrees of
freedom. This explains why pure soft-mode and pure local-flip coordinates can each miss the
coupled reaction path.

Interpretation
--------------
e564 is the first project state that gives a direct, one-local-topology-event bridge from a
C3-derived minimum to the exact defect composition of the known D2 20-dislocation family.
The direct local route is now strongly excluded, not merely weakly sampled. The frontier has
therefore shifted to coupled coordinates and coordinated relaxation: global C3-breaking
shear plus localized scar shortening, scaffolded environment repair, and the global e564-D2
basin interface. If these all close without a new 20-dislocation minimum, the evidence will
support a stronger conclusion that the 21->20 transition is intrinsically collective rather
than a local edge-flip event.
