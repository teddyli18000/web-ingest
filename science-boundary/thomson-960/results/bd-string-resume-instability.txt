Interpretation
--------------

transition path does not exist.  The earlier small-step corrected string was
moving in the right direction but very slowly; simply increasing eta destabilizes
the discretized string.  Do not spend more compute on identical long explicit
string continuations.

smallest sampled C negative-doublet amplitude 0.0015, phase 40 deg relaxes to B

bracketed inside a 5-degree interval in the two-dimensional unstable manifold.
