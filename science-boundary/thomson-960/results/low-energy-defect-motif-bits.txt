Scientific implication
----------------------
The meaningful reduced coordinate is not 'scar family versus a separate twin
family'.  The actual low-energy landscape already mixes conventional and
twinned charge-+1 defect units.  A better search representation is therefore a
12-motif discrete/continuous hybrid:

- binary motif adjacency (whether each defect unit contains a 7-7 edge),
- motif identity / local 5<->7 rewrites when a unit changes type,
- global placement/orientation/shear coupling between the twelve units,
- C3 symmetry groups the twelve units into four 3-cycles at symmetric states.

The live record R has not yet been assigned a verified twin-bit pattern in this

Queued N=972 and scar-to-twin workflows remain scientifically useful, but their
interpretation must change.  They are searches over motif-count/pattern and
