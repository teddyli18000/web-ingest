An earlier version of this note incorrectly treated the current low-energy
R/A/B/D/C scar family as if it had no 7-7 twinned cores.  Direct spherical
Delaunay analysis of the saved A/B/D/C coordinates disproves that premise.
The merged-shot shorthand '12 scars 5-7-5-7-5' does not imply that the two
heptagons are never adjacent elsewhere in the full Delaunay graph.

For A/B/D/C, the 60 non-sixfold sites split exactly into twelve disconnected
five-vertex defect components.  Each component has 3 degree-5 + 2 degree-7
vertices and four 5-7 edges.  Some components additionally contain the 7-7 edge
that defines the historical Wales twinned motif.  Measured twin counts are:

  A  444368.39568622573   9 twinned components
  B  444368.40297286137   8 twinned components
  D  444368.41099308454   4 twinned components
  C  444368.41141788290   3 twinned components (C is index-2)

Every observed 7-7 edge has exactly three peripheral pentagons.  Full details,
including exact edges and the C3 12-bit motif representation, are in
`results/low-energy-defect-motif-bits.txt`.

The live record R has NOT yet had its twin count measured in this durable note.
Do not infer it from energy, C3 symmetry, or shot prose.  The queued direct-live
flip workflow will measure `initial_twin_count` when Actions scheduling resumes.

Historical motivation
---------------------
Wales & Ulker, Phys. Rev. B 74, 212101 (2006), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevB.74.212101,
describe the same local twinned motif: two seven-coordinated sites share a
Voronoi/Delaunay edge with three peripheral pentagons, net topological charge
+1.  N=912, 932 and 972 are explicit examples.  Wales, McKay & Altschuler,
Phys. Rev. B 79, 224115 (2009), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevB.79.224115, further analyze
large-N defect motifs around this size regime.

The historical N=972 minimum remains especially useful, but now as a source of
a different *motif pattern / global defect organization*, not as a wholly new
binary topology family absent at N=960.  Its reported coordination is
36 pentagons / 912 hexagons / 24 heptagons, while the N=960 low-energy states are
36 / 900 / 24.  Thus the 12-particle size difference can in principle be
absorbed entirely by deleting twelve sixfold bulk sites while leaving the full
5/7 defect count unchanged.

The same 2006 work reports a low-barrier N=972 transition between twinned and
conventional grain-boundary minima (about 0.00424 / 0.00104 in the two
directions).  This is evidence that different local motif organizations can be
nearby on the large-N landscape; it does not justify treating the twelve N=960
motifs as independent binary variables without further tests.

Revised reduced representation
------------------------------
The useful discrete coordinate is now:

- twelve charge-+1 defect components;
- for each component, whether its two heptagons have an internal 7-7 edge;
- the exact local 5<->7 identity rewrite when a component changes motif;
- the placement/orientation/shear coupling among all twelve components.

At C, C3 symmetry groups the twelve components into four 3-cycles.  In C-aligned
component order, the measured low-energy motif bits are:

  C = [111 | 000 | 000 | 000]  (3 twins)
  D = [111 | 000 | 000 | 010]  (4 twins)
  B = [111 | 010 | 110 | 110]  (8 twins)
  A = [110 | 010 | 111 | 111]  (9 twins)

C->D is almost an elementary motif rewrite: 11/12 defect components keep the
same five point labels; one component changes from conventional to twinned with
only four coordination-class changes (602 5->6, 822 6->7, 831 6->5, 919 7->6),
creating twin edge (822,842).  This makes a defect-level rewrite search much
more concrete than generic coordinate kicks.

Queued cross-size searches: revised interpretation
---------------------------------------------------
The existing N=972 workflows remain scientifically useful but must be interpreted
as *motif-pattern / defect-organization propagation* rather than preservation of
an otherwise absent topology.

1. Historical motif diagnostic
   `code/analyze_twinned_motif.py`, run `40798144771` for N=912/932/942/972.
   It will measure 7-7 counts and component structure for direct comparison with
   A/B/D/C and, eventually, R.

2. Bulk-only 972->960 propagation
   `code/propagate_twinned_family.py`, run `40798582516`.
   It excludes the non-sixfold defect core from particle deletion and gives beam
   priority to descendants retaining 7-7 edges.  Its scientific role is to keep
   a historically distinct high-twin/defect pattern alive during the 12-particle
   size change, not to introduce the first twin at N=960.

3. One complete 12-point tetrahedral orbit removal
   `code/search_972_twin_orbit_removal.py`, run `40798729324`.
   N=972 -> 960 is exactly 12 particles and the historical parent is T_h.  A
   generic proper-T orbit has 12 points.  Removing a bulk sixfold 12-orbit maps
   36/912/24 exactly to 36/900/24 before relaxation while preserving the global
   symmetry skeleton.  This finite discrete construction remains a genuinely
   different search interface from sequential greedy deletion.

Direct N=960 motif rewrite search
---------------------------------
`code/search_scar_to_twin_flips.py`, queued run `40799277860`, should likewise be
reinterpreted.  Because the live state may already contain twins, its objective
is to find local Delaunay flips that *increase or rewire* 7-7 motif adjacency
while preserving the 36/900/24 coordination histogram, not to create the first
twin from a twin-free state.

A coverage-safe BFS front-end, `code/search_scar_to_twin_flips_bfs.py`, records
states explored by flip depth and an explicit truncation flag.  Do not launch it
as a duplicate until the queued pilot has actually executed; use it if the pilot
needs a coverage-controlled follow-up.

Decision rule
-------------
- Any N=960 relaxed child below `live_score-2e-7` triggers immediate live-record
  re-check, stable repolish, rationalization, official verifier and tools/check.py.
- Otherwise compare each child by the full twelve-component motif pattern, not
  just energy or total twin count.  Near-degenerate structures with different
  motif placement must not be deduplicated solely by energy.
- Failure of one 972 propagation or one short flip-BFS is evidence only about
  that search interface, not proof that a given motif pattern is impossible.
