Thomson N=960 — force-norm refinement negative result on shallow C-down branches
Date: 2026-10-29

Question
--------
Could the Amore-style stationary-point reformulation V=1/2||g||^2 efficiently
refine the two C-down near-index-1 branches that have the correct Morse index but
physical tangent gradient RMS ~3e-4 to 4e-4?

Method
------
Used `code/refine_stationary_force_norm.py` mathematics locally on the already
downloaded run-40735119192 artifacts.  The exact spherical metric derivative in
dV/dq was included, rigid-rotation leakage was projected, and matrix-free
centered finite-difference Hessian-vector products supplied H g.  This was a
numerical-interface test only; no candidate score was inferred from it.

p100 branch
-----------
Initial:
  E = 444368.40388814337
  physical gradient RMS = 3.60468894e-4
  lowest curvature ~ -0.00668914
  H g RMS = 2.272754e-2

After 100 L-BFGS iterations minimizing V:
  E = 444368.4038881443 (scientifically unchanged)
  physical gradient RMS = 3.60417891e-4
  H g RMS = 1.1566e-3

The optimizer made H g small while leaving g essentially unchanged.  This is the
expected nonzero/near-null local-minimum failure mode of the force-norm
objective near an almost-flat Hessian direction.

p0 branch
---------
Initial:
  E = 444368.4038646134
  physical gradient RMS = 4.19966394e-4
  lowest curvature ~ -0.0927087
  H g RMS = 7.21157e-1

After 50 L-BFGS iterations:
  E = 444368.40386456635
  physical gradient RMS = 3.66373751e-4
  lowest curvature after the short refinement ~ -0.11596
  H g RMS = 1.8987e-2

The force decreased only ~13% and H g was non-monotone.  This is far too slow to
justify scarce Actions runner time relative to overlap-tracked eigenvector/
subspace following.

Decision
--------
Do not launch a dedicated force-norm Actions batch for these C-down shallow
separatrix seeds.  Retain `refine_stationary_force_norm.py` as a diagnostic or
for seeds where the Hessian is well-conditioned, but use overlap-tracked
index-1 / CHiSD-style dynamics for the present shallow branches.  In particular,
small H g is not sufficient evidence of stationarity when g remains nonzero.
